## Reading
- Every CREPE size is under the 15 s target on this Mac; `full` is 9.7-9.8 s, `medium` about 3.3 s, pYIN 0.6-0.7 s. The target is for the laptop, which
  was not measured: a Windows laptop may be slower, so `full` has little margin. The default CREPE size is `medium`.
- Matching is the same or marginally worse: pYIN matches 102/119/62 notes with 2/2/0 missed; CREPE medium and full give the same matched and missed notes
  but more extras (video1 15 and 14 vs 11; video2 9 and 8 vs 4). The 20-note spot check is identical (20/20, 19/20, 20/20) for all.
- The Ode to Joy score was written from pYIN's own transcription, so that row favours pYIN; it cannot show CREPE winning.
- No accuracy gain, and CREPE needs TensorFlow, so the recommendation is to keep `TRANSCRIBER=pyin` as the default and offer `crepe` as an opt-in.
