The Concert, Marching and Beginner coaches now restate their own priorities last in every feedback, plan and chat prompt, so the same measured problem is framed and drilled differently by each coach (phrasing and long tones, pulse and marking time, or one plain correction and three steady notes). Chat replies, "Practice plan" included, no longer retell the plan step by step or start with "Then…" or "Finally…".
