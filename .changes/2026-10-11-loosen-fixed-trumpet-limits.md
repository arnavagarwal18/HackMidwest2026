The fixed posture limits for "shoulders raised" (front) and "elbows held wide" now sit outside the range of good players: neck ratio below 0.15 instead of 0.30, and elbow gap above 1.3 shoulder widths instead of 1.0. Good players were being flagged by the old limits; the rules that follow a take's own starting posture are unchanged.
