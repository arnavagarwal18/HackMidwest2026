The take screen is now a simple Play screen: a tempo stepper, an "Add camera" switch and one big Record button, with a line explaining why the microphone is needed. The microphone and camera choices are remembered, the microphone is opened without pitch-altering processing, and permission problems now explain how to fix them in Safari or Chrome. Calibration is optional and reachable from a "Fine-tune" link.
