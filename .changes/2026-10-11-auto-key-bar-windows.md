When a take comes back as "I couldn't tell", the app now tries every key from -12 to +12 (starting with the one your notes point to), and if none fits it sends the same recording again for the first and second half of the bars and shows the one that matches, with a line such as "Matched bars 1–8 at -4 semitones."
