While a take is being checked you now see the steps (Uploading, Finding notes, Matching key and bars, Writing feedback) with a Cancel button and a Try again after a timeout. The results lead with one sentence and separate pitch and rhythm scores, show what the app heard (key and bars) with a Change option, explain "I couldn't tell" with one next step, and make Play again the one main button.
